How to use the NC Tracker

A one-page guide for Heads of Department, nominees and Action Owners.

Report a problem For HoDs and nominees For Action Owners What the statuses mean
{# --- 1. Reporting --------------------------------------------------------- #}

Found a problem? Tell the NC Manager

A non-conformity (NC) is anything that doesn't follow a policy, procedure, standard or regulation. You don't need the app to report one: send it to the NC Manager by email, message or in person, and they will record it.

Please include:

  1. What happened, and which rule or procedure wasn't followed
  2. Which department should fix it (it can be your own)
  3. Which area or process it affects, e.g. fee collection or examinations
  4. When it was found, and who found it
  5. How it was found: daily work, an internal review, monitoring / KPIs or feedback
  6. A photo, record or email as proof, if you have one

The NC Manager gives it a number such as NC-{% now "Y" %}-001 and sends it to the department that must fix it. Your department is told when it has been logged.

{# --- 2. HoDs --------------------------------------------------------------- #}

For Heads of Department and nominees

  1. You're told about a new NC for your department by email and in the app (the bell at the top). Open it from the message or from My tasks.
  2. Within {{ validation_days }} working days, decide if it is valid. (Working days are Monday to Friday, not counting public holidays.) Click Yes, it is valid, or Mark as not valid and give your reason. If you say it isn't valid, the NC Manager decides and you are told the outcome.
  3. Choose an Action Owner from your department to fix it (or do it yourself). They are told straight away.
  4. Agree the action plan: four short items: the root cause, the corrective action, the Action Owner and a target date.
  5. Keep an eye on progress in My tasks, the Register and the Dashboard.
You can see every NC across APIIT, but you can only change your own department's. Going on leave? Ask the NC Manager to set an HoD nominee, who can do all of the above for you.
{# --- 3. Action Owners ------------------------------------------------------- #}

For Action Owners

  1. You're told when you are made Action Owner. The NC appears in My tasks.
  2. Fill in or update the action plan: root cause, corrective action and target date.
  3. Add progress notes as you go, so everyone can see where things are.
  4. Need more time? Use Change target date and give a reason. The original date is kept on record.
  5. When the action is done, add evidence (a file or a link, with a short description), enter the date it was completed and click Send for verification.
  6. The NC Manager checks it. Either it is closed, or it comes back to you with comments explaining what is still needed. Fix it and send it again.
Reminders: you get a reminder {{ reminder_days }} days before the target date. If it passes, you and your HoD are told, and after {{ escalation_days }} days overdue the NC Manager is told too.
{# --- 4. Statuses ------------------------------------------------------------ #}

What the statuses mean

Pending validationWaiting for the HoD to say whether it is valid
DisputedThe HoD says it isn't valid; the NC Manager will decide
ValidConfirmed; waiting for an action plan
In progressThe corrective action is being carried out
Pending verificationDone, with evidence; waiting for the NC Manager to check
ClosedChecked and effective. Locked.
Not validThe NC Manager agreed it isn't valid. Kept on record, not counted as open.

Overdue a deadline has passed · Due the target date is within {{ due_soon_days }} days · no flag: on track or closed. NCs are never deleted.
